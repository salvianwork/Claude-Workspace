# Vertical Short-Form Phase — Agreed Notes

**Status:** Decisions gathered in conversation (2026-11-24). This is the next phase to design.
Read this file at the start of the vertical-phase design conversation.

## Goal
Make videos for **TikTok and YouTube Shorts** (the owner's main targets).

## Agreed so far
- **Format:** 1080×1920 vertical becomes the default. Landscape stays an option for
  regular YouTube.
- **Layout:** redesigned for vertical, not cropped.
  - Taller framing of places; the action sits in the middle.
  - The host stands lower and partly in the scene.
  - The camera moves more vertically.
- **Safe zones:** captions and labels stay out of the areas the apps cover (the right-edge
  buttons, the bottom caption and username, the top bar). The checker tests this
  automatically, like the face-overlap check.
- **Short recipe for Quill:**
  - target 45–75 s;
  - a hook in the first 2 s;
  - one idea every 5–8 s;
  - an ending that invites a rewatch or a comment.
- **Places:** most of them need taller versions.

## Host "phone-ready" refresh (owner approved, 2026-11-24)
- Head about **20% bigger**. The same face design, so the character stays recognisable.
- Bolder eyebrows, eyes and mouth shapes, so expressions read at phone size.
- Softer body shapes: a slight waist taper, a less boxy casual sweater, softer and bendier
  arms.
- **No glasses (owner decision 2026-11-24)**, and skin #F0CDAE. The host's face is curly hair, no glasses.
- Show the owner a **before/after sheet first**, and wait for approval before applying.
- Optional, to consider in design: a real three-quarter torso (non-symmetric lapels) for
  close-ups.
